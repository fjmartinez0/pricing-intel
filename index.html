Pricing Roles Research

Pricing, monetization and RGM roles in Canada, Colombia and the United States, and what the hiring says about each company's pricing strategy.

Only verified roles are shown: the employer's own posting was checked and open. Leads that can't be confirmed yet, often because the only source is LinkedIn, stay hidden until an employer posting is found, and close automatically after 14 days. First seen is when this tracker first saw the role, not when the employer posted it. Strategy notes are inferences from job postings, not confirmed company plans.
